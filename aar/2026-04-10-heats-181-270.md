## Lessons Learned

1. **Clear design specs = fast implementation.** Chisel's ASCII wireframes eliminated 80% of design decisions during coding.
2. **Variable shadowing is real.** The `p` → `prio` bug (heat 229) cost a full debugging heat. Static analysis would have caught it.
3. **Route ordering matters in FastAPI.** `/review/play` must precede `/review/{subject_id}` — learned in heat 193.
4. **Python 3.9 compatibility.** `dict | None` type hints fail on 3.9; use `Optional[dict]` or no annotation.
5. **Dispatch under-budgeting works.** Giving 33 heats and using 17 means 16 heats for self-directed polish — which produced real value (weekly chart, streak, session memory, deck view).
