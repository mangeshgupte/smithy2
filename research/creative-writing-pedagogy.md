# Creative Writing Pedagogy — 5th Subject Design

## What Makes a Good Writing Tutor?

### Core Principles
1. **Writing is revision** — first drafts aren't the product, the revision process is
2. **Show don't tell** — teach through examples and counter-examples, not rules
3. **Constraints breed creativity** — prompts with specific constraints produce better writing
4. **Feedback on specific elements** — "the dialogue feels stiff" beats "needs improvement"
5. **Read to write** — analyzing good writing teaches you to produce it

### Exercise Types That Work for Text-Only

| Type | How It Works | Tutor Exercise Type |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Prompt response** | Write 2-3 sentences given a specific constraint | free_response |
| **Identify the technique** | Read a passage, name the literary device used | multiple_choice |
| **Rewrite** | Given weak writing, make it stronger | free_response |
| **Fill the gap** | Complete a sentence with the right word/phrase | fill_in_blank |
| **Analyze** | Explain why a passage works or doesn't | free_response |

### Proposed Topic Structure (5 levels, 10 topics)

**Level 1: Foundations**
- Show Don't Tell (replace "she was angry" with action/detail)
- Strong Verbs (replace weak verbs with specific ones)
- Sensory Details (engage the 5 senses)

**Level 2: Sentences**
- Sentence Variety (short/long, simple/complex rhythm)
- Eliminating Wordiness (cut unnecessary words)

**Level 3: Style**
- Voice & Tone (formal/informal/playful/serious)
- Figurative Language (metaphor, simile, personification)

**Level 4: Narrative**
- Dialogue (natural-sounding, reveals character)
- Scene vs Summary (when to slow down, when to speed up)

**Level 5: Craft**
- Opening Hooks (first lines that grab attention)

## Why Creative Writing Over Science

- **No visuals needed** — purely text-based exercises
- **Perfect for Socratic** — "Why does this version work better?" / "What would happen if you removed this detail?"
- **Multiple valid answers** — encourages creative thinking, not just recall
- **Pairs with English Vocab** — vocabulary fuels better writing
- **Universal skill** — benefits students, professionals, anyone who writes

## Exercise Design Notes

- free_response exercises need flexible validation — AI (Claude) evaluates quality
- Provide a "model answer" for self-comparison, not exact matching
- Include "before/after" examples in concept sections
- Socratic questions should probe *why* a choice works, not just what the choice is
